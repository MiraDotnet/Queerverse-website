How to apply

We're a small whitelisted server, so new people join in small groups.

You don't have to be a pro builder. Just be nice, be yourself and actually want to play with us.

  1. Join the Discord

    discord.gg/Dn2MnPvpuB

  2. Apply there

    Send your application on the Discord.

  3. Have a quick chat

    If it looks like a good fit, we'll message you on Discord to say hi.

  4. Get whitelisted

    If it's a good fit, we add you to the whitelist and you can join at queerverse.net.
