Getting started

First time on? Do these and you're good.

  • Join with queerverse.net on Java Edition 26.3.
  • Set your pronouns with /pronouns she/they (or whatever yours are). They show when people hover your name in chat and in the tab list.
  • Check out your profile card with /profile. Click it to add a little bio, or use /profile bio your text here. /profile Name shows someone else's.
  • Want to build away from spawn? /rtp drops you somewhere random in the wild.
  • Say hi when someone new joins! You get a little sparkle for it.

Claiming land

Plugin: Lands

Claims keep your stuff safe. It's free.

  • /lands opens the Lands menu, where you can do pretty much everything.
  • Make your land with /lands create Name, then stand in a chunk and use /lands claim to claim it.
  • You can claim up to 50 chunks, and you can add friends to your land from the menu so they can build with you.

Gaterunes (fast travel)

Our own way to travel. Build a rune stone at your base and you can teleport between your runes.

  • Place a lodestone on top of a diamond block. It turns into a gaterune stone.
  • Right-click a rune to open the travel menu. Right-clicking someone else's rune attunes you to it, so you can travel there too.
  • /gaterunes opens your runes from anywhere, and /gaterunes journal shows your milestones.
  • In the menu you can rename your rune, pick one of the styles (pride flags, cherry torii gate, amethyst geode and lots more), sort them into folders and pick favorites. Some styles only show up during their season.
  • Travel has a 3 second warm-up and a short cooldown. You can have up to 5 runes.
  • To pick it back up, hit your own rune three times quickly and you get both blocks back.

Artisan's Chisel (decorations)

Turn any block or item into a decoration you can move, turn and resize.

  • Craft it with an amethyst shard + a stick.
  • Hold the chisel in your main hand and a block or item in your off hand, then right-click a surface. It becomes a decoration.
  • Right-click a decoration to grab it. Hold right-click on an arrow to move it, on a ring to turn it, or on the white cube to resize it (from tiny up to 5 blocks big). Let go to place it. Sneak for bigger moves or finer turns, and left-click to undo.
  • Sneak + right-click a decoration for its menu: exact turns, size, full brightness or remove.

Villagers

No more pushing villagers around with boats.

  • Sneak + right-click a villager with an empty hand to open its ledger.
  • From there you can see its trades, open trading, or pick it up and carry it over your head. Sneak + right-click the ground to set it down.
  • Reweave Fate gives it brand new trades, but only before anyone has traded with it (same rule as normal Minecraft).

Shulker boxes

Plugin: ShulkerAnywhere

Open them without placing them.

  • In your inventory, right-click a shulker box to open it.
  • Or hold one and right-click the air.

Trees and ores

Plugin: TreeTimber, VeinMiner

Less grinding, more building.

  • Trees: chop one log and the whole tree comes down. Sneak while chopping if you only want to break that one block.
  • Ores: mine one ore and the whole vein breaks at once.

Sleeping

Nights go by fast here.

  • Only 30% of the people online need to sleep to skip the night.
  • Need it to stay night (mob farm, spooky build)? /nosleep asks everyone not to sleep. Use it again to take it back.
  • Days are twice as long as normal, so you get more building time. Nights stay the normal length.

Handy commands

Plugin: EssentialsX

The everyday stuff.

  • /sethome, /home, /delhome for your homes, and /spawn to get back to spawn.
  • /tpa Name asks to teleport to someone, /tpahere Name asks them to come to you. They answer with /tpaccept or /tpdeny.
  • /back takes you back to where you were, even where you died.
  • /msg Name hi sends a private message, /r replies.
  • /mail send Name text leaves a message for someone who's offline.
  • /warp lists the warps, /afk marks you as away, and /helpop text pings the admins.

Extras

Plugin: CreeperGuard, DiscordSRV, Emotecraft, PatPat

Small things that make the server nicer.

  • Creepers can't blow up your builds.
  • Chat is linked with our Discord, so you can talk to people in game from Discord and the other way round.
  • Optional mods: if you install Emotecraft you can do emotes, and with PatPat you can pat people and pets. Nobody needs them to play, but they're cute.
