Rules

mia: this is a starting draft. Reword these however you want them to sound.

  1. Be kind.
    No bigotry of any kind: homophobia, transphobia, racism, sexism, ableism, all of it. This one is zero tolerance. You get removed, no warnings.
  2. Use people's pronouns.
    They're on the Discord roles and on /pronouns in game. If you get it wrong, correct yourself and move on.
  3. Don't grief or steal.
    Don't break, take or change other people's stuff without asking. We log everything with CoreProtect.
  4. Pranks are fine if they're harmless.
    Nothing that destroys builds, wastes a lot of someone's time or can't be undone. If they wouldn't laugh, don't do it.
  5. Ask before building close to someone.
  6. No cheats.
    No hacked clients, x-ray or duping. Ask if you're unsure about a mod.
  7. Keep it age-appropriate.
    mia: say which age range the server is for.
  8. Tell a mod if something's wrong.
    In game or on Discord. You won't get in trouble for asking.
